BIOHAZARD – Chapter 4

Kaoru: Why would we take part in an experiment that sounds like something a cartoon villain would do…?

Tomoya: “If we could create duplicates of people, or rather idols, in this case, there would be various benefits.”

Hajime: “Fundamentally, one person can only do so much. They cannot be in multiple places at once nor complete multiple jobs at the same time.”

Tomoya: “However, if there are clones such as ourselves, such scenarios would become possible.”

Hajime: “For example, suppose there is a super idol who is talented across the board-”

Tomoya: “This idol would be able to simultaneously sing and dance on stage, star in a movie, take part in a gravure photoshoot, recount a funny story on a variety show, all at the same time.”

Hajime: “In other words, things that would otherwise be impossible, become possible.”

Tomoya: “Even if the original person grew old or passed away, their duplicates could continue delivering consistently outstanding performances.”

Hajime: “24/7, 365 days a year!”

Tomoya: “Without suffering from mental health problems or making a single complaint.”

Rei: I see... Truly the ideal employees…

Robots have no human rights. Naturally, there are costs involved in manufacturing and maintaining them, but there's no need to pay them a salary.

From a manager's perspective, duplicates are probably far more convenient than real idols, who will inevitably grow weary and reach their limits.

Koga: That’s fuckin’ insane, man. Is Es… Is ES heartless or somethin’?

Rei: Nay, I imagine Itsuki-kun would say that twisting the world to satisfy one’s own desires is precisely what makes us human.

Kaoru: Itsuki-kun? Isn’t that more of a Tenshouin-kun thing to say?

Rei: His philosophy on art is surprisingly unconventional, you see. I would know, since we are friends.

Adonis: Fumu… Personally, I find this way of thinking difficult to accept, but I understand the general idea.

This technology is most likely an extension of such things as AI art generators. If we reach a point where things superior to real, flesh-and-blood humans can easily be mass-produced—

What do we do then?

AI already possesses skills that could take us years or even decades of hard work to master.

And AI can accomplish it with a simple press of a button.

At first, our feelings of revulsion towards these “monsters” will most likely outweigh everything else.

Since existing laws weren't designed to account for non-human beings, they would probably face restrictions, much like AI-generated art does.

But as generations pass, the legal framework develops, and this kind of technology becomes commonplace—

Will there still be a place for us, for real human beings, in that world?

Koga: W-what’s gotten into you, Adonis? You’re bein’ weirdly talkative.

Adonis: I’ve been thinking about it since Yuuki introduced me. I’m not very good at thinking these sorts of things through, so I am yet to come up with an answer.

Rei: Adonis-kun is a thoughtful boy.

In my opinion, if that is the direction the world is moving in, I’d rather go along with it. There’s little point in throwing your dollies out of the pram and insisting you don’t like it. We cannot turn back the tide of such a huge movement.

Even if we’re repulsed by the idea and refuse to participate, you’ll simply approach other idols instead, won’t you?

Hajime: “Yep.”

Tomoya: “We don’t particularly need UNDEAD’s help.”

Koga: Oi! What are you tryna say, huh? I thought you were supposed to be all cute and innocent!

Tomoya: “We are aware that our words and actions are uncharacteristic of the original Ra*bits.”

Hajime: “Even with a generous estimate, our level of resemblance to the originals is only around 40%”

Tomoya: “Even that figure is based mainly on physical resemblance. Our resemblance to them internally is considerably lower.”

Rei: You are using rather complicated language unbefitting for Ra*bits.

Hajime: “That's right. To reproduce your inner selves, that is, your psychology, personalities, and mental states, we need to obtain data from your brains.”

Tomoya: “And that is what we are requesting of you for this experiment.”

Hajime: “We're going to attach specialised equipment to your heads, or more specifically, your brains.”

Tomoya: “Then, by extracting as much data as possible from your brains and using it to recreate you–”

Hajime: “We believe we'll be able to manufacture idols who are the "same" as you, not only in appearance but also on the inside.”

Kaoru: A-are you going to suck all the data out of our brains? That's a little creepy, isn't it?

Hajime: “That's a rather misleading way of putting it..”

Tomoya: “We're simply collecting data. There's no possibility of any lasting damage to your brains or minds.”

Hajime: “Of course, all your personal information will be fully protected in accordance with the law.”

Kaoru: …

Hajime: “Well, if you don’t want to, you can always refuse.”

Tomoya: “If you do, we will make the same request to other idols, such as Ra*bits, to participate in your place.”

Hajime: “However, the experiment itself will not be cancelled.”

Tomoya: “The AIIE Project will continue until we're able to manufacture the perfect artificial idol.”

Rei: …
