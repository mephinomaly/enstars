BIOHAZARD – Chapter 5

Time: That night

Location: In the accommodation facilities on the grounds of the AIIE experimental facility

Rei: Yaaay! A sleepover with the whole of UNDEAD~♪

Fufu. The SS preliminaries were such a wonderful opportunity for us to spend time together, but poor Kaoru-kun ended up getting stranded out in the desert, leaving us all scattered to the four winds.

I'm positively overjoyed that the four of us can finally gather under one roof for a lovely little pajama party... Whoa!?

Koga: …

Rei: Koga? You shouldn't go throwing manju at me, you know? Or is this your way of saying you'd like to play catch?

Koga: Don't you dare act all carefree, you vampire bastard! What the hell d'you mean, "pajama party"?

Rei: Oh? Such a nostalgic way of addressing me. Could it be that you've been longing to call me that again? Shall I start calling you "Wanko" once more, for old times' sake?

Koga: It's not like we're all buddy-buddy again, dumbass! I'm callin' you that to insult you!

Rei: Goodness, what am I to do with all this abuse? Don't go taking out all your pent-up frustration on poor little me.

After all, we all agreed to take part in this experiment, did we not?

Surely it's a little late to start voicing our objections now, wouldn't you say?

Koga: If anything, they practically forced us into it! If we hadn't agreed, they'd have gone and used completely innocent people like Ra*bits as their test subjects instead, wouldn't they?!

Then we'd hafta sit back and watch those guys become the victims, knowing full well we could've done somethin' to stop it!

...I'd rather throw myself right into the thick of things. At least then I can live with myself.

But don't get the wrong idea. I'm still not okay with any of this. Got that?

Kaoru: Ahaha. It definitely sounds shady, and it's pretty unsettling, but it's also pretty interesting as a concept, don't you think?

Artificial idols and all that... Man, science and technology have really come a long way. It's just like something out of an old sci-fi movie.

If this were just a movie, or, well, if it were someone else's problem, I might actually be able to enjoy it without a care in the world.

Adonis: I feel somewhat uneasy about it too, but from the perspective of scientific and technological progress, I find it fascinating.

The future I imagined when I first learned about Voc*loid and AI-generated art is now unfolding right before our eyes.

We're living in the kind of near-future sci-fi world that people in the past could only imagine.

It's almost like a dream, for better or worse.

It makes my heart race.

Kaoru: Ahaha. I suppose there's something rather boyish and romantic about it, isn't there?

I'm not opposed to that sort of thing, either. After all, I'm the son of a scholar who ventured out to sea in pursuit of romance and mystery.

Adonis: I'm not opposed to exploring unfamiliar fields, either.

Rei: Speaking practically, if we'd turned down this request, our agency might well have given up on us, considering the slump we're currently in.

Rhythm Link has a wide roster of idols to choose from, after all, so they're not exactly sentimental when it comes to these things.

If we look at it as an opportunity to prove our worth and avoid being cast aside, then participating is a necessary step. It seems we'll also receive a rather substantial payment, so there's a clear financial incentive as well.

Well, we made quite a fortune during the SS, so we're practically rolling in money at the moment.

And if this experiment involving artificial idols succeeds, is put into practice, and is eventually announced to the public, then we'll attract more attention ourselves as the idols who served as its test subjects. It's a net positive for us, wouldn't you say?

Koga: But this isn't gonna solve the actual problem, is it?! An experiment like this is just a way of buyin' ourselves some time! It's got nothin' to do with the problems we're facin'!

This better not turn into some wild goose chase, with us runnin' around like idiots and gettin' nowhere!

Rei: Indeed. We should all take our time and think of ways to address the underlying problem Koga has brought up. Fortunately, we'll have plenty of time to do just that.

Kaoru: Mmm... I was kinda on my guard when they first mentioned an experiment, but if all they're asking us to do is stay here for a while...

Rei: Indeed. We'll be given medication that will keep us asleep for roughly half the day. Whilst we're sleeping, data will be collected through the devices attached to our heads.

When we're awake, we'll exercise to keep our bodies from getting sluggish and eat regular meals to stay in good health. The four of us will simply spend our time together, getting along as pleasantly as can be.

The experiment will last about a week. Apparently, any unnecessary stimulation could interfere with the results, so we're not allowed to do any other work during that time.

Our phones and other devices will be confiscated, and we'll be completely cut off from the outside world.

Kaoru: I feel like a hospital patient or a prisoner... Well, if we're getting paid just to laze around, I suppose it's an easy enough job.

Rei: Indeed. We may as well take it easy. Apparently, we'll be paid whether the experiment succeeds or fails, so it's not as though we'll come away empty-handed even if it doesn't work out.

Fortunately, it seems our accommodations will be considerably more comfortable than those of a hospital or prison.

Look, they've even provided playing cards and board games to help us pass the time. Let's all play together until the experiment begins—or, rather, until it's time for us to go to sleep.

Koga: Don't you think you're lettin' your guard down a little too much? Honestly...

Rei: What's wrong with that? I've always been strangely mature for my age, ever since I was a child, so I never had many opportunities to enjoy things like this. Having a sleepover with friends my own age, for instance...

So, even though I have no idea what the future holds, I'm having a rather wonderful time right now ♪
