BIOHAZARD – Chapter 6

Koga: (…)

(...Hng? Huh?)

(Damn, is it mornin’ already?)

(The sun’s so bright… I musta slept like a log without dreamin’ ‘cos of those sleeping pills I had t’take.)

(The second I closed my eyes, it was morning already. Feels like I haven’t slept at all.)

(Wait, what? Why’s the sun so fuckin’ bright—!?)

(What’s happenin’!?)

(Where am I? I swear I fell asleep in some ryokan…?)

(The hell am I lying outside for? This is so fuckin’ weird!)

(Shit? I can’t move my body!?)

(Is this sleep paralysis or something? What the fuck’s happenin’!?)

Keito: –Oogami!

Koga: (Hnn, is that…?)

Keito: I know you’re up there! Come down, I have something I need to discuss with you!

Koga: (That ain’t Hasumi-senpai, that’s that four-eyed bastard. Gross, he’s still got that annoyingly nice voice!?)

Keito: Oogami! At least respond! What are you doing on top of the bike shed? I don’t know if you’re trying to imitate Sakuma-san or what, just stop with these bizarre antics!

Koga: Haaa? Shut up, you loser. This is the quietest and sunniest place t’sleep!

(Huh? My mouth’s movin’ on it’s own! This feels so fuckin’ weird!?)

Keito: Who are you calling a loser? You clearly have no idea how to talk to your upperclassmen, do you?

Well, I suppose it's better than having you awkwardly tiptoe around me… Right, I guess I’ll just-

Koga: Damn, you just climbed onto the roof. Who woulda thought Mr Honour Student had the balls t’do somethin’ like that.

Keito: Hm. When I was a child, I was quite the mischievous little brat myself. I would climb onto the main temple building and kick the Buddha statues, and every time, my parents or older brother would give me a rap on the head.

Koga: That’s nothin’ t’be proud of ya know.

Keito: Well, yes, I know… All imitating Sakuma-san and acting in such a reckless manner will accomplish is making other people dislike and criticise you. Nothing good will come of it.

Koga: I’m- I’m not tryna imitate him! This is jus’ the best place t’nap!

Keito: Are you unable to sleep properly at home? Do you have a bad relationship with your family or something?

Koga: M-My relationship is fine! All they’ve done is start lecturin’ me for goin’ to the underground live house!

Keito: Well, that is where hooligans tend to gather. I understand how your parents must feel. They’re lecturing you because they’re worried.

Koga: I-I'm not some little kid who needs his parents worryin’ about him anymore!

Keito: No matter how old you are, you will always be a child in your parents’ eyes.

Hm… The view from here is surprisingly good.

Oogami, look. On the horizon– is that a boat?

Koga: Don’t know don’t care.

Seriously, dude… Whaddya want with the great Oogami Koga, Hasumi-senpai?

Keito: “The great Oogami Koga”

Koga: T-the hell you complainin’ about?

Keito: No, nothing. I hope that doesn’t make for an embarrassing memory down the line.

Koga: You use kisama, practically the same thing.

Keito: How annoying. Anyhow, back to the topic at hand. As for why, I couldn’t say, but Sakuma-san has asked to meet us to discuss some Deadmanz related business.

He told us to come to the livehouse as soon as we can after school ended.

Koga: Haa? Deadmanz related business?

Wasn’t Deadmanz just a temporary unit for that one performance the other day?

Keito: That was my understanding as well. But it was necessary for us to establish Deadmanz as an official unit, and we still haven't completed the procedures to disband it.

So, at least officially, both you and I are still members of Deadmanz.

Kiryu, who helped out with that abhorrent Deadmanz live, was counted as a guest, and therefore was not an official member.

Koga: Ah, right, ‘course. That scary lookin’ guy…

Wait, so does that mean I get t’stand on stage again as Sakuma-senpai’s partner?

Yahoooo! Then, fuck man! We gotta go, shitty glasses!

Keito: Don’t badmouth my glasses.

It reminds me of how Sakuma-san and Eichi wouldn't stop teasing me when I first started wearing glasses as a child. Just thinking about it makes me angry.

At any rate, to be perfectly honest, I'd rather never go through that again... I need to change my way of thinking, change my strategy, and set about reforming Yumenosaki.

I have a mountain of things to do and think about, and I'm already busy.

And being summoned by Sakuma-san is bound to mean trouble. If I could, I’d pretend I didn’t hear anything and just go home.

It can’t be helped. I set a rule for myself that a unit member must always follow their leader’s instruction, so I will do as such.
