PSYCHOBREAK — Chapter 10

Time: One week later. The day before the “Vampire’s” subjugation battle, named Flashback (tentative name)

Location: The day UNDEAD first met. In Hasumi Keito’s family’s temple graveyard.

Rei: Bein’ a ghost must be fun ♪ Bein’ a ghost must be great ♪

Don’t have to work, don’t have to study, you can just have a concert in a graveyard from dusk ‘til dawn~♪

Adonis: What’s wrong, Sakuma-senpai? Are you drunk?

Rei: I'm not old enough to drown my sorrows in alcohol. Though maybe things would've been easier if I were ♪

Adonis: I'm not sure why, but you're in unusually high spirits... You've seemed rather gloomy lately, so I'm a little relieved to see you like this.

Rei: Fuwah. That’s amazin’, that’s amazin’! You really noticed I'd been feelin' down?

No matter how much people say they like or respect someone, they don't usually pay much attention to others, let alone understand how they're feelin'.

Adonis: I've simply known you longer than the others, Sakuma-senpai, so I noticed the change in you a little sooner.

Rei: Oh, don't be so modest. Even my little brother, who's been with me since the day he was born, hasn't got a clue what I'm goin' through!

I even pull these attention-seekin' stunts, goin', "Hey, hey! Do you know what your onii-chan's thinkin' right now...?" Just 'cause I want him to understand how I feel.

Adonis: Is that why he thinks you're annoying and hates you?

Rei: H-he doesn't hate me! The Bible says a little brother loves his big brother just as much as his big brother loves him!

Adonis: I see. Since my understanding was incorrect, I apologise.

Rei: Adonis-kun, anyone ever told you you’re a joke killer?

Adonis: Killer...? I've been trying to keep a low profile so that I don't earn a shameful nickname like that.

I am a foreigner. I stand out in this country whether I like it or not. Sadly, people often take issue with me for no reason. Even mothers out with their children sometimes seem strangely wary of me.

That's why I think I ought to be especially meek and well-behaved - a serious, harmless, good boy.

Rei: Hmmm. You've got eyes that see more than most people's, Adonis-kun. And because of that, you end up seein' things you might be better off not seein'.

How could I ever think (you), of all people, were the one behind the "vampires"?

Things have been particularly bad lately, but I've had this really unsettling feelin' that someone's been steering my thoughts.

It's like I'm bein' forced to play a part in some story written by someone I don't know the first thing about.

Is it just a puberty thing? Whaddya think, Adonis-kun?

Adonis: I don't know. But I do think that accusing me of being the culprit and denouncing me was rather out of character for you.

Fortunately, it seems the suspicion surrounding me is beginning to lift, but honestly, I was a little hurt.

Even you misunderstood me.

Rei: Ah, yeah. Sorry about that. I really do love you, y'know?

Adonis: I'm not talking about whether you like me or not. I'm talking about how you see me and whether you understand who I am.

Koga: ...What're you two talkin' about?

Weren't we supposed to be practisin' for our showdown with those "vampires" today...? And that... what was his name? That Hakaze guy hasn't even shown up.

And just when I thought you'd finally turned up, Sakuma-senpai, you're still over there with... uh...

Adonis: I am Otogari Adonis. I have told you my name numerous times.

Koga: Adonisu… What’s this got to do with him?

Is this really gonna be okay? Can we even beat those "vampires"? Of course, if Sakuma-senpai's here, we're invincible, but—

Rei: Realllyyy now. Maybe I’ll betray you again like I did at the Deadmanz live, hm?

I don’t really like it when people depend on me. Doesn’t really do it for me ya know.

C’mon, Wan-chan, if it makes you nervous, tell me somethin’ inspirational. Somethin’ that’ll get me going ♪

Koga: I dunno if this'll work, but...

What was it called, DreFes? I figured we wouldn't get anywhere if we didn't have at least a few songs to perform, so... I tried writin' a few songs myself.

Rei: Really now? Impressive! You're startin' to look like a proper band member~♪ Go on, let's hear it~♪

Koga: W-well, they're not very good, so don't get your hopes up, okay?

I only started dabbling in songwriting as a hobby a few years ago, and I'm self-taught, so I'm still not all that good at it...

Rei: What’s with the sudden politeness? Cut it out. You're not actin' like yourself.

Back at the Deadmanz live, you were bitin' and snappin' at me, weren't ya?

Koga: I was all worked up back then... But now that we're in a band together and we've gotten closer, somehow you seem even more amazing to me.

I-I'm nervous... Sorry. If you don't like it, I'll go straight back to acting all tough and aggressive.

Rei: Interestin’ choice of words... Hmm. Hm hm?

Ah, yeah. Let's hear these songs of yours, yeah?

♪~♪~♪
