In Memoriam◆Handwriting, Chasing, Rambling

Good-Bye / Chapter 1

Time: At the same time

Location: In the Seisoukan common room

Kaoru: —So Hasumi-kun did end up catching us and kicked us out.

He was all like, “This isn’t the place for idle chatter. How incorrigible.” Then he gave quite the lecture.

Rei: We were the only people there. He could have shown us a little mercy.

Good grief. Hasumi-kun is too much of a stickler for the rules.

Kaoru: Ahaha. He seems perfectly happy when we use the room for book club, though…

…Maybe it’s just because you’re there. You two are such good friends that he feels like he has to give you a hard time.

Rei: Eeh~? Wouldn't that be all the more reason for him to be kind to me?

I can’t exactly deny that though. Hasumi-kun is hardly the sort to play little tricks on people.

My apologies for dragging you into this, Kaoru-kun.

Kaoru: No, you’re fine. I was just kinda rambling anyway.

Anyhow. I’m thirsty so I’m gonna get something to drink. Want some coffee?

Rei: Green tea for me, please. And perhaps some salty rice crackers too…♪

Kaoru: You’re pushing your luck, gramps. I’ll boil the water, but you’re on your own after that.

Rei: Hmph, so heartless. Very well, I suppose I shall endure a cup of coffee.

Kaoru: You should have said that at the start.

Rei: Well, yes, but there’s always a chance you’d take pity on me and make it for me, hm?

Kaoru: What, brew you some green tea?

That “what if” isn’t gonna happen ♪

Anyway. I wonder if Adonis-kun has found Koga-kun. I’ve not heard anything from them though.

Rei: Are you worried about those two?

Kaoru: Not particularly. They’re not children, they can look after themselves.

I’m wondering if it’s possible for Adonis-kun to find him without having any sort of clue where he went.

Rei: This is Adonis-kun we’re talking about, of course he can.

Kaoru: Yeah? What makes you so sure? Gut feeling?

Rei: That is certainly part of it but once he has made his mind up about something, he is not one to be easily swayed.

He’s a sweet boy. He’d happily take a step back for the sake of his friends. But when it comes to Koga, that is another matter entirely.

No matter how long it will take, he will most certainly find him. That’s simply the sort of person Adonis-kun is.

Kaoru: Fufu, you’re right.

So his favourite artist passed away…

I might sound a bit cold-hearted saying this but…

When I see on the news that some celebrity has passed away, it always feels like it has nothing to do with me.

I mean, we’re all part of the same industry, but you can’t know everyone, right? Especially if you’re from different generations. There’s plenty of people whose names you know but you’ve never actually met.

So I can’t really say I understand how Koga-kun feels…

…Though, I suppose my situation was different.

When my mum died, I did the same thing. I couldn’t stay there, so I ran away.

I was just a kid, and there weren’t many places I could even go. I only lasted about half a day before I had to go home.

I just… didn’t want to be there. No matter where I looked, mum wasn’t there.

No matter how long I waited, she wasn’t going to come back.

She’d never sit by my side again, telling me stories about creatures in the ocean as she tucked me into bed.

She’d never say “goodnight” to me again. Or “good morning”.

It was horrible. All these things that had always seemed so obvious… Suddenly, I had to face the fact that they were never going to happen again.

But it was when we started preparing for her funeral and wake that it really began to sink in.

“Mum can rest in peace now” I’d say. I’d pretend I understood. I’d smile like I did at least.

But truthfully, I didn’t believe it. Not even for a second…

It felt like I’d been thrown into the cold depths of the ocean. Like it hurt just to breathe.

Rei: …

Kaoru: …And I’d find myself thinking “a week ago today, she was still with us.”

Then, slowly, over the course of a week, that feeling would change.

“A month ago today” then “a year ago, around this time”. Even now, when I think of her, my chest gets all tight.

I'm pretty used to life without mum now. Most of the memories I have of her are lighter these days.

But… I wish I'd spoken to her more, I wish we'd spent more time together.

When I was a kid, she'd take me by the hand. But now I wish I could take hers and bring her anywhere she wanted to go.

I've even got my boating license now. I can show her the beautiful ocean… take her anywhere she wants…

Those are the kinds of “what ifs” that keep coming back to haunt you.

Rei: It's very clear that you loved your mother dearly.

Kaoru: Love, huh…

You really don't think about things like that until someone’s gone.

I took it for granted that mum would always be there. That's the truth of it.

…The only reason I'd smile was so people would leave me alone.

If a tiny kid bravely tells you they're okay, what more can you say to them?

Everyone was dealing with their own things. They didn’t exactly have the time to watch over me.

So I'd always say I was okay.

It never crossed my mind that I could let someone else share the burden with me.

…Haha. Sorry, I didn’t mean to start talking about myself like that

Rei-kun's probably been through way worse than I have. Now that I’ve said all that, I'm starting to feel a bit embarrassed.

Rei: Kukuku. Grief is not something that can be measured by how eventful or uneventful one’s life has been. Everyone has a story of their own.

I, too, have lost someone dear to me. Someone utterly irreplaceable.

You mustn't compare your grief to that of another. There is no measure by which one person’s sorrow can be deemed greater than another’s.

Koga is still lost within that whirlwind of grief.

Given his temperament, I’m afraid that anything we might say could only serve to distress him further.

Though, I suppose I have little room to speak, considering I allowed Adonis-kun to go after him alone.

Kaoru: He might not listen to us. but maybe Adonis-kun’s straightforwardness will get through to him.

Guess we'll have to wait. Nagging isn't exactly going to change his mind.

We’ll just have to give him the time he needs. Wait until Koga-kun can find his footing again, and then keep marching forward…

Rei: Yes, let us.

Koga will find his way back in time. And when he does, he will take hold of his own life once more.
