Good-Bye / Chapter 5

Rei: Naturally, his frustrations grew. In his youth, he did not yet know how to express them properly.

He would drown himself in alcohol and clash with those around him. His behaviour was often far from praiseworthy.

And so, before long, the public’s perception of him began to change.

The young man who had once been that generation’s darling gradually found himself branded a problem child - a troublemaker.

He was cast as the villain no matter what he did. If he remained silent, he was accused of being complicit. If he spoke, his words were twisted and he’d be accused of something else entirely.

He was hounded by the press and plagued with sensationalist rumours. His frustrations only mounted. It was an endless, vicious cycle.

When we reunited for the first time in quite a while, he seemed absolutely exhausted…as though he were weary from the simple act of living.

So I told him, “there’s no reason for you to keep paying attention to England and all their antics, ya know?”

He had broken free from the confines of his rural hometown and made a name for himself on a global scale. If he’d managed to do that once, I thought, surely he could do it again—only this time, somewhere farther afield.

So I suggested he try performing in Japan.

Koga: That was you…?

Rei: Indeed. Though, I must say, establishing oneself and performing from the ground up in an unfamiliar land is no small undertaking, is it?

In those early days, I lent him what assistance I could. Finding venues, making arrangements… things of that nature.

Koga: …But you didn’t have to pick a livehouse like that.

He was world-famous. There had t’be better options. A place like that was way too small for a rockstar of his fame.

Rei: Kukuku. One would certainly think so. But that particular request came from MARBLING himself.

He told me he wanted somewhere small. A venue reminiscent of those he played in the days before his name carried any weight, before he had amassed a large audience.

He had stood beneath the lights of countless enormous arenas by then. Yet this time, he wished to perform somewhere he could look out from the stage and see every face in the audience. That is what he told me, at least.

Koga: …

Rei: As it happened, he grew rather fond of such places.

He would return to Japan time and time again, and whenever he did, he continued to seek out venues of that nature. He performed in them right up to his very last show.

There was something to be said about having an audience so close. Seeing their faces illuminated by the stage lights, watching them become utterly captivated by the music before them… And then there were the young musicians who would occasionally share the stage with him.

I believe he was rather enamoured with that energy, of those who were still standing at the very threshold of their own journeys. It reminded him that he still had something to prove. ”I can’t allow myself to be outdone just yet,” he would tell himself.

…Mind you, simply finding a new stage did not mean that all the regrets he carried from his life had suddenly vanished.

Koga: Regrets?

Rei: Yes. In his youth, success was the only thing he cared for.

He was so afraid of losing everything he had built that he could not bring himself to leave his agency.

And that was not all. There were more than a few occasions when he came to realise he had wandered down the wrong path, yet by then, he could no longer bring himself to turn back.

Some of these choices would cost him things truly precious to him. Even decades later, whenever those memories resurfaced, he would speak of them with the same old regret.

Koga: …

(...I see. So gramps had things he regretted too. Things that can’t be changed, no matter how badly you wish they could be.)

(To me, he always seemed like someone far beyond my reach. Like he was somewhere way above the clouds…)

(But of course… he’s a human, jus’ like me. Flesh and blood. Jus’ like me.)

…Hey, Sakuma-senpai.

Do you know…do you know what he actually died of…?

…Actually, nevermind. Forget I asked.

Whatever you say, it won’t change anything.

Whether it was illness or an accident… however he died, it has nothing to do with what he gave me.

His voice moved my soul. And that’s not somethin’ that simply disappears overnight.

His music, his soul… they’re not gone. They're still alive in me.

Rei: …Kukuku.

Koga: Oi, why’re you laughin’?

Rei: Nothing. I was merely thinking that the Koga I know has finally returned.

…In that case, there is one last thing I wish to ask of you.

Koga. And what, pray, will you offer him in return?

Time: A few days later

Location: In the Band Rehearsal Studio

Koga: —Here you go. I printed enough copies for everyone, so pass ‘em around.

Adonis: Rambling Moon... is that the name of our new song?

Koga: Yeah. Wrote the whole thing myself.

Rei: Ohoho, Koga…♪

Koga: Don’t look at me like that. Whatever you’re tryna say, jus’ save it and take a look.

Adonis: …I see. This is somewhat unexpected.

Since Oogami wrote it, I was expecting something more intense. More aggressive. Something with a stronger rock sound.

But this melody feels rather whistful…yet gentle, with a warmth underneath it.

Koga: Yeah? You think so?

Kaoru: You put all that work into it, and that’s all you’ve got to say?

Koga: C’mon, man. It’s not like I sat down ‘n thought “I’m gonna write somethin’ different today.”

I jus’ wrote down whatever was goin’ through my head.

Kaoru: I see. But that’s a good thing, isn’t it?

I’m sure the fans will be thrilled to see us pull off a song like this ♪

Koga: Yeah… probably.

Rei: Hm? And what is this meek little expression, hm?

Koga: …Nah, it's just– Back then, I wanted nothin’ more but t’be exactly like you.

I tried to copy everythin’. From the way you sang, the way you threw yourself around the stage… all of it.

But it’s different now. I’m not some kid who's jus’ chasin’ after Sakuma-senpai anymore.

I’ve been standin’ shoulder to shoulder with you as a member of UNDEAD for a long time now.

So what’s the point in imitatin’ you forever?

I’m gonna find a sound that you couldn’t make. Somethin’ only I can. And I’m gonna turn it into a weapon for UNDEAD..

That’ll be a hell of a lot more interestin’ from now on, won’t it?

…This song’s the first step towards that, I think.

Adonis: Yes. If you create music that only you can make, then UNDEAD will become even more stronger than before.

Koga: Yeah. I dunno yet what it’ll take t’knock his socks off but…

I’m gonna blow you away someday, so you better be ready, Sakuma-senpai…♪

Rei: Sniffle sniffle. Are we not friends? Why must you treat me as if I’m your sworn enemy?

Anyhow. I admire this spirit of yours. I’ll be counting down the days until that time arrives ♪

Koga: Tch. You won’t be able to keep actin’ all cocky for long, buddy.

But uh, before we get started…

Adonis: …That guitar pick?

Koga: Yeah. It’s one from gramps’ show. The one I managed to catch.

It means a lot to me, so I don’t wanna break it… I’m only using it for the first note. For you, gramps.

♪~...

—Let’s go!

Oi. How long are you guys gonna stand around lookin’ stupid! Let’s get this show on the road!

Kaoru: We were waiting for you though?

Koga: Does it matter!? C’mon, get in position!

Adonis: Fufu… The usual Oogami is back.

Koga: Yeah! Sorry to keep you guys waitin’!

Follow my lead! Rock'n'roll......!
