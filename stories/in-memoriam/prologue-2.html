In Memoriam◆Handwriting, Chasing, Rambling

Prologue 2

Time:Several hours later

Location:The green room of the Middle School Band Contest

Adonis: We can rest here for a bit whilst the points are being totalled up. Well done on your hard work, Oogami.

Koga: You too, Adonis.

Ah shit, my back is killin’ me from that chair. If I don’t get up ‘n move I’m gonna get all stiff.

Adonis: Perhaps it is because you were sitting completely still, intensely watching the stage.

…I’ve brewed some tea, Oogami. Drink up.

Koga: Thanks man…Fuah, feelin’ better already ♪

I said I wasn’t gonna go easy on these kids jus’ because they’re kids but damn, they sounded way better than I thought they would.

The performances could do with some work and you could tell they were tryna cover up their mistakes but you could feel every bands’ passion.

Adonis: Umu. I’m not an expert but I was deeply moved by the sight of them playing their instruments with passion.

You could really feel that everyone wanted to share their music with the world.

That’s why it’s difficult to decide who should win. Which band should receive the “Otogari Adonis Award”...

Oogami, what criteria are you using to decide? I’d like to hear your thoughts.

Koga: Mmm. I’m not sure either.

If the criteria is based on metrics like fewer mistakes or playin’ well, then you can have a machine figure it out.

Things like does it reflect current trends or does it appeal to the audience – that will be reflected in the audience vote.

But more importantly, since they asked us specifically, that means they wanna hear things that numbers alone can’t decide.

So I’m gonna decide based on how much of the person I can see comin’ through the music.

Whether you call it your soul or your way of life, it doesn’t matter. I don’t care if it’s awkward soundin’.

I wanna hear the music that only that person can make. That’s rock.

Adonis: I see.

You do always say that rock is about the soul.

Koga: Yup. I wanna leave a mark on this world, proof that I lived. That’s why I make music, rock’n’roll…♪

Adonis: I think Sakuma-senpai was right to leave this job to you.

You’ve approached this in a more honest way than anyone else could.

Today’s contest may be over but their music will continue to ring out.

I don’t know what you will say exactly, but I’m sure your words will become a source of encouragement​ for them.

I’m proud of you, Oogami.

Koga: Here we go again with you sayin’ somethin’ so brazen ‘n blunt.

You were in charge of judgin’ as well. I’m jus’ takin’ this seriously cos otherwise I’ll look lame as hell.

We’re gettin’ off track. But, do you get what I’m sayin’?

Adonis: Umu. I like the idea of deciding based on how much of the performer’s personality the music has.

Anyhow. What did you think of entry number 12? You were rather interested in them earlier.

Koga: Number 12…? Ah, those guys?

They did sound like MARBLING. The way the bass sounded, you can tell they did their homework.

But…it wasn’t enough.

Adonis: Wasn’t enough? As in, the technology?

Koga: Nah. If we’re talkin’ jus’ musical performance, those kids actually did better.

They probably like the music that MARBLING was puttin’ out when he first debuted.

His music was flashy, that sort of “I won’t take shit from adults” kind. Not scared of anythin’ or anyone.

But that’s not the music I’m familiar with.

His music is the kinda music you can only make if you’ve lived a long life.

Well, they’re only middle schoolers. Probably askin’ too much of them.

MARBLING was their inspiration to start their band, but they aren’t outright copying him.

Adonis: Fumu…I’m starting to get curious with how much you’re talking about this person.

We still have some time. I’ll do a little bit of research.

Koga: Research? On your phone? You never do that.

Adonis: I’m not very good at typing with my fingers but there’s this useful feature where you can search using your voice.

Even I can manage that.

— MARBLING, rock, music.

Koga: Oioi. Is this some ancient googlin’ method? You don’t hafta do it like that.

Adonis: It’s not a problem. It knows what I’m talking about.

…Videos and news articles have come up.

…?

Koga: What’s up? Readin’ about one of his heroic stories from when he was younger?

Adonis: No…not quite…

…

Koga: Adonis?

What’s with the silence? And that weird expression?

Adonis: …Oogami. I want you to be strong for a moment.

The rockstar you admire has—

He passed away this morning.

Koga: …What?

…Gyahaha! Nice try, Adonis.

That old man’s a legend, people have been sayin’ he’s dead or he’s gonna die for ages.

Every time you look up his name, “death” and “cause of death” always come up in the suggested searches.

It’ll jus’ be another one of those shitty rumours.

Adonis: That's all well and good but…

It’s not just news articles. Multiple different sources are saying the same information.

Koga: Tch… Let me have a look.

It’s probably jus’ some shitty Japanese gossip sites—

(...It’s not.)

(These are real articles.)

(It’s not jus’ music sites postin’ about it… public broadcasting sites are too.)

…Nah, this has got to be a mistake of some kind.

Adonis: Oogami…?

Koga: There’s no way he’s died.

He’s stronger than anyone else. He’s even more energetic than young people.

…

…We should hurry up ‘n decide on who wins.

We’re in the middle of a contest. We haven’t got time to be distracted.

Adonis: …Right.
